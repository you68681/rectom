**Chao Lei, Guang Hu, Meng Yang, Yanbei Jiang, and Nir Lipovetzky**  
*Mind the Perspective: Let's Reason Recursively for Theory of Mind*  
Findings of the 2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2026 Findings)

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11724

## Overview

This work studies Theory of Mind reasoning in large language models under partial and asymmetric observations. We introduce **RecToM**, a recursive perspective-reasoning approach that uses explicit representations of agents' perspectives and belief states to support reasoning about nested beliefs across multiple agents.
